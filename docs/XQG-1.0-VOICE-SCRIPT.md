# Xen Quality Guardian 1.0 — Approved Voice and Caption Script

## Wrong answer

“That answer is not correct yet. Your work is saved privately on this device. You can
correct and retry, hear a coaching hint, or continue and revisit this activity before
the lesson can be completed.”

## Deferred activity

“Checkpoint saved. This activity is still required. Warden will bring you back before
completion.”

## Recovery failure

“This control did not produce the expected result. Your work remains saved. Pause the
lesson and report the visible control label—do not share your private answer.”

Captions must always display the complete script. Spoken guidance may be stopped or
replayed and never activates a microphone.
