# Speak Spanish Inside Xen Academy

Xen Academy now supports permission-gated spoken Spanish practice inside Daily Bread.
Listen at slow or natural speed, record only when you choose, replay your attempt, receive
focused word and pacing feedback, retry without penalty, and resume from locally saved
progress.

Audio is never uploaded or retained. The browser recording is temporary and can be
deleted, cancelled, or disabled at any time. When voice is unsupported, typed Academy
activities remain available.

Assessment uses browser speech recognition and local scoring. It reports phrase
completion, word accuracy, pacing, fluency, hesitation, and a clearly labeled
recognition-derived pronunciation proxy. It does not claim phoneme-level pronunciation
analysis.
