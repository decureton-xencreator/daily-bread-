# XAVR 1.0 - Approved Voice and Caption Script

All narration must use the approved Xen voice identity when available. The visible caption
is authoritative.

## First-run guidance

“Welcome to Xen Academy voice practice. Your microphone is off. Read the privacy note,
listen to the model phrase, and press Start Speaking only when you are ready. Your
recording stays in this browser and is never uploaded.”

## Recording

“Listening. Speak naturally, and take the response time you need. Press Stop Recording
when you are finished.”

## Review

“Your microphone is off. Replay your attempt, retry it, delete it, or submit it for
assessment.”

## Passed

“Passed. Your best attempt has been saved locally. Continue when you are ready.”

## Focused retry

“Let’s focus on one correction. Listen to the model, repeat the highlighted word, then say
the complete phrase again. Retries do not reduce your score.”

## Denied or unavailable

“Microphone access is not available. You can change this site's microphone permission and
retry, or continue with typed Academy work.”

## Privacy controls

“Recording deleted.”
“Voice disabled. Typed Academy activities remain available.”
“Local learner data reset.”
