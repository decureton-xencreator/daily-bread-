Warden fails closed on premature lesson completion and exposes the telemetry boundary in diagnostics. XEW may use aggregate findings to propose refinements; it does not silently publish personal telemetry.

Cross-device Academy state, remote analytics, calendar-derived schedule enforcement and raw behavioral surveillance remain not implemented.
