# XDBS Guided Deck + Spatial Integrity Sync

Date: 2026-07-24  
Release: XPS 4.3.0

## Inherited outcome

Daily Bread now treats spacing as a governed component contract. Text, metadata and controls must remain in document flow, wrap safely and retain readable separation at every supported viewport.

## Product tutorial

The six-stage tutorial explains navigation, Academy Resume Anywhere, Globe orientation, verified source actions, Mission Control and Warden/XER privacy. It launches on first use, remains replayable, moves the underlying scene to match each lesson and records only aggregate local tutorial events.

## Warden gate

- No absolute-positioned card actions may overlap descriptive copy.
- All compact controls must wrap and remain touch-accessible.
- Tutorial controls must be functional, keyboard reachable and dismissible.
- Tutorial completion remains local; it is not transmitted.
- Reduced-motion and privacy contracts remain binding.
